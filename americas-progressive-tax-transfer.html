Tax Foundation — America's Progressive Tax and Transfer System (2019)

Federal vs state & local effective fiscal incidence rates by quintile, before and after transfers

This chart recreates Table 3 of the Tax Foundation's March 2023 report "America's Progressive Tax and Transfer System: Federal, State, and Local Tax and Transfer Distributions" (Vermeer, Durante, York, Walczak). They model the total US fiscal burden — federal + state + local — and present it as effective fiscal incidence rates by income quintile, both before and after netting transfer receipts from income.

The "before transfers" panel matches more closely with how PSZ, ITEP, and CBO present total tax rates (gross taxes / income). The "after transfers" panel shows the redistributional effect of transfer programs — the bottom-quintile rate falls from 24.6% to 10.1%, while the top-quintile rate rises from 34.5% to 41.4% because transfers reduce the denominator faster than taxes reduce the numerator.

Scope note. Tax Foundation's measure is federal + state + local, all taxes (income, payroll, corporate, property, sales, excise, estate). Comparable in scope to ITEP and PSZ; broader than the federal-only series (Splinter NTJ, CBO, TPC). The "after transfers" version is unique to this paper among the dashboard charts.
