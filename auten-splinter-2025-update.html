Top 1% implicit effective tax rate, robustness across methodologies

Derived from Auten & Splinter (2025 update), Table 1: top 1% pre-tax vs after-tax shares

This chart derives an implicit top-1% effective tax rate from Table 1 of Auten & Splinter's July 2025 update to their JPE 2024 paper. AS's Table 1 publishes top 1% pre-tax and after-tax income shares under six sequential methodological adjustments to their 2024 baseline. The implicit ETR for the top 1% is computed as (pre - post) / pre for each (year × adjustment) cell.

The headline finding: the implicit top-1% ETR is remarkably stable across all six adjustments. In 1979 it sits at ~22% regardless of which methodology is applied; in 2019 it sits at ~36% across the board (range: 35.95% to 36.45%). That is, AS's tax-rate conclusions about the top 1% don't depend on which of these reasonable methodological choices you make.

Derived measure. The values plotted are not directly published as effective tax rates in AS-Update-2025 (the paper publishes income shares). They are derived from the published pre-tax and after-tax share columns. The derivation is exact arithmetic, not estimation; it relies on AS's tax measure (federal + state + local) and income measure (pre-tax/after-transfer national income) being applied consistently across both share series — which they are by AS's construction.

Each pair of points (1979 + 2019) is one methodological adjustment. The tight clustering shows AS's top-1% tax-rate conclusion is robust to the alternative assumptions tested in the update.

How this chart was produced. Values read programmatically from AS's published AS-Update-2025.xlsx; ETR derivation is arithmetic — no estimation.
Show transformation steps
  • Read sheet T1 of .raw/auten-splinter-2025-update/AS-Update-2025.xlsx via openpyxl.
  • Extracted top 1% pre-tax share (col B for 1979, col E for 2019) and after-tax share (col H for 1979, col K for 2019) for rows 5–11.
  • Computed implicit ETR = (pre - post) / pre × 100 for each (adjustment, year) pair.
  • Row 9 ("Revised NIPA data") has --- placeholder for 1979 in source; that point is omitted (only 2019 plotted for that adjustment).
  • Output: data/auten-splinter-2025-update/top1-implicit-etr.csv + metadata.json.
