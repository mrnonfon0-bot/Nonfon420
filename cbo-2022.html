Average federal tax rates by income group, 1979–2022

Congressional Budget Office — The Distribution of Household Income, 2022

This chart shows the average federal tax rate paid by each income group every year from 1979 to 2022, straight from the CBO's flagship distributional series (released January 2026). The rate is total federal taxes — individual income, payroll, corporate income, and excise — divided by income before transfers and taxes, computed as an income-weighted average within each group.

The system is clearly progressive: in 2022 the average federal rate ran from 1.4% for the lowest quintile to 25.9% for the highest quintile and 31.5% for the top 1%. Two features stand out historically: the long decline in the lowest quintile's rate as refundable credits (EITC, child tax credit) expanded, and the dramatic negative rates for the bottom in 2020–2021, when pandemic recovery rebates and the expanded child credit exceeded the taxes those households owed.

This is the same CBO source that underlies the Splinter (2020 NTJ) quintile chart elsewhere on this dashboard — extended here from 2016 out to 2022.

Scope: federal taxes only (income + payroll + corporate + excise) as a share of income before transfers and taxes (market income + social insurance benefits). This excludes state & local taxes — so it is narrower than the ITEP and PSZ all-government charts, and directly comparable to the other federal-only series here (Splinter NTJ, TPC).

Sources — every line, with exact citation

  • CBO (2026): Congressional Budget Office, "The Distribution of Household Income, 2022," publication 61911, January 2026. cbo.gov/publication/61911.
  • Data file: CBO supplemental data, table 09 "Federal Tax Rates" (households ranked by income before transfers and taxes), households_ranked_by_inc_before_trans_tax_table_09_federal_tax_rates_1979_2022.csv. Each line on this chart = the federal_taxes (total) rate for one income_group, household_type=all_households.
  • Definitions: Average federal tax rate = total federal taxes ÷ income before transfers and taxes, income-weighted within each group. Federal taxes = individual income + payroll + corporate income + excise (≈90% of federal revenue). Income groups contain approximately equal numbers of people. Per CBO's README and report.
  • Public domain: CBO works are not subject to copyright; figures reproduced here match CBO's published values (2022 anchors: 1.4 / 13.4 / 25.9 / 31.5%).
