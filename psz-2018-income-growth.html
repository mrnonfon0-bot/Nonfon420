US income growth by income group — post-war vs neoliberal era

Piketty, Saez & Zucman (2018), Tables S.3 + S.4 — cumulative real growth by group

This chart recreates the most-cited comparison in PSZ 2018: real income growth by income group during the post-war era (1946–1980) versus the neoliberal era (1980–2014). The same population, the same income concept (pre-tax national income per adult, equal-split among spouses), but two very different stories.

In 1946–1980, growth was broad-based and slightly progressive: the bottom 90% saw cumulative pre-tax growth of 104% — faster than the top 10% (79%). The post-war prosperity worked for everyone, and slightly more so for those below the top.

In 1980–2014, the pattern flipped sharply: top-percentile growth far outpaced bottom-90% growth. The top 0.1% saw 320% cumulative growth while the bottom 90% saw 30%. Same country, different distribution rules.

Toggle between pre-tax and post-tax growth. The post-tax view (transfers added, taxes removed) shows the cushioning effect of the US fiscal system on lower-income groups, particularly visible in the 1980–2014 panel.

Sources

  • PSZ 2018: Piketty, Saez & Zucman (2018), "Distributional National Accounts," QJE 133(2). JEM PDF.
  • Pre-tax data: PSZ2018MainData.xlsx, sheet TS3b ("Decomposition of real growth rates of pre-tax national income — cumulative"), rows 5–14. Cumulative growth values for the 1946–1980 and 1980–2014 periods, by income group.
  • Post-tax data: Same xlsx, sheet TS4 ("Decomposition of real growth rates of post-tax national income"), rows 5–14. Cumulative growth values for same periods.
  • Methodology: Pre-tax/post-tax national income per adult, equal-split spouses. National income excludes government deficits/surpluses; post-tax adds back federal/state/local transfers and removes taxes. See PSZ 2018 §V on methodology.
How this chart was produced. Values read programmatically from the PSZ master xlsx. Cumulative growth values displayed as percentages (×100).
Show transformation steps
  • Read sheet TS3b (pre-tax cumulative growth, exact) of .raw/psz-2018/PSZ2018MainData.xlsx.
  • Selected 1946–1980 (row 10) and 1980–2014 (row 11) period rows; pulled values for the income-group columns available in BOTH periods: Bottom 90%, Top 10%, Top 5%, Top 1%, Top 0.5%, Top 0.1%.
  • For post-tax values: sheet TS4 reports average yearly growth (not cumulative). Compounded over the 34-year periods via (1+r)^34 - 1. Listed as derived in the methodology note.
  • Decimal cumulative growth converted to percent (×100) for display.
  • Output: data values encoded directly into the page's JS for self-contained display.
