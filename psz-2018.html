Total effective tax rate by income group — Piketty, Saez & Zucman (2018)

All US taxes — federal + state + local + corporate + property — by income group, 1962 / 2010 / 2014

This chart recreates the income-group-level effective-tax-rate data from Piketty, Saez & Zucman's 2018 Distributional National Accounts. The bars show total taxes paid as a fraction of pre-tax national income for each income group, broken into six tax types (sales, residential property, payroll, income, corporate & business property, and estate).

Toggle between 1962, 2010, and 2014. The change in overall progressivity, the rising share of payroll taxes at the bottom, and the fall in the estate tax at the top are all visible from the same data.

Read this before comparing to other dashboard charts. PSZ uses an all-government, all-tax measure — federal + state + local, income + payroll + sales + property + corporate + estate. This is broader than Splinter, CBO, TPC, and Auten-Splinter, who publish federal-only rates. The PSZ levels will look higher than the federal-only series for that reason. Compare like-for-like before drawing conclusions.

Sources — every bar segment on this chart, with exact citation

  • PSZ 2018: Piketty, Saez & Zucman (2018), "Distributional National Accounts: Methods and Estimates for the United States," Quarterly Journal of Economics 133(2), 553–609. Paper PDF.
  • Data sheet: PSZ2018MainData.xlsx, sheet TS6 — "Taxes paid by pre-tax income group." Three year-panels (1962 starting row 4, 2010 starting row 14, 2014 starting row 24). Available from PSZ's project page: gabriel-zucman.eu/usdina. Data appendix PDF.
  • Methodology: Tax incidence assumptions (employer-side payroll borne by workers, corporate tax incidence per PSZ assumptions, etc.) are documented in PSZ 2018 §IV and the data appendix §A.4. Differences in these assumptions are what drive disagreements with Splinter/Auten-Splinter.
How this chart was produced. Values read programmatically from the TS6 sheet of PSZ's published PSZ2018MainData.xlsx — no pixel tracing.
Show transformation steps
  • Read .raw/psz-2018/PSZ2018MainData.xlsx, sheet TS6, using openpyxl.
  • Pivoted three year-panels (1962, 2010, 2014) from wide to tidy long format (year, income_group, tax_type, rate).
  • Decimal fractions converted to percentages for display (×100).
  • Output: data/psz-2018/taxes-by-income-group-and-type.csv + metadata.json.
