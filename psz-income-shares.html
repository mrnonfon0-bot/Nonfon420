US pre-tax income shares by income group, 1913–2019

Piketty, Saez & Zucman — Distributional National Accounts (2022 update of 2018 QJE)

This chart shows what share of total US pre-tax national income went to each income group, every year from 1913 to 2019. Data comes from the PSZ 2022 update of their 2018 QJE estimates — the authoritative Piketty-Saez-Zucman series, computed on an equal-split-adult basis (one adult = one observation, spouses' income split equally).

The PSZ story: top-10% share fell from ~46% in the 1920s to a low ~33% in the late 1970s, then rose back to ~46% by 2019. The mirror image is the bottom 90% share, which rose to ~67% in the post-war period then drifted back to ~54%. The famous "U-shape" of inequality is built from these series.

Click any series in the legend to hide/show it. Start by comparing the Top 1% (the most-cited inequality number) against the Bottom 50% (the disappearing middle-and-bottom).

Hover any point for the exact year and share. Toggle series in the legend to compare specific groups.

Sources — every line, with exact citation

  • PSZ 2018 (paper): Piketty, T., Saez, E., & Zucman, G. (2018). "Distributional National Accounts: Methods and Estimates for the United States," Quarterly Journal of Economics 133(2), 553–609. JEM PDF.
  • PSZ 2022 update: Updated estimates through 2019 on the authors' project page: gabriel-zucman.eu/usdina/.
  • Data file: PSZ2022AppendixTablesII(Distrib).xlsx, sheet TB1 ("Shares of total pre-tax income, equal-split individuals, % of national income"). Every series on this chart maps to one column of that sheet.
  • Methodology: Pre-tax national income measure (broader than fiscal income — includes corporate retained earnings, payroll-tax-paid wages, employer-paid health insurance, government interest, etc.). Equal-split-adult population basis. See PSZ 2018 §III and the data appendix on the project page for details.
How this chart was produced. Values read programmatically from PSZ's published PSZ2022AppendixTablesII(Distrib).xlsx sheet TB1 via openpyxl. No pixel tracing.

  • Downloaded PSZ2022AppendixTablesII(Distrib).xlsx from gabriel-zucman.eu on 2026-05-28.
  • Read sheet TB1 "Shares of total pre-tax income (equal-split individuals)" via openpyxl. 108 year-rows (1913–2020); 2020 is blank in source.
  • Decimal fractions in source converted to percentages for display (×100).
  • Bottom 50% / Middle 40% only populated from 1962 onward in PSZ 2022; earlier values are blank in TB1.
  • Output: data/psz-2022-update/pre-tax-shares.csv + metadata.json.
