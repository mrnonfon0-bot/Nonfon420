US income shares, pre- vs post-tax: top 10%, 1%, bottom 50%

RealTime Inequality (Blanchet, Saez & Zucman) — equal-split adults, year-end, 1976–2024

US income shares from RealTime Inequality, December values 1976–2024. Solid lines are pre-tax national income; dotted lines are post-tax income (after taxes and transfers). The gap between a group’s solid and dotted line is what the tax-and-transfer system redistributes.

Taxes and transfers pull the top 10% / top 1% down and lift the bottom 50% up — but the post-tax top shares still sit well above their 1970s levels.

Equal-split adults, year-end (December). Pre-tax = pre-tax national income; post-tax = post-tax income (after all taxes and transfers). Built from user-supplied Full Database.xlsx (RealTime Inequality structure); owner to confirm exact version.

Sources — every line, with exact citation
