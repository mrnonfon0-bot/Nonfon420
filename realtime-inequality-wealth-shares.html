RealTime Inequality (Blanchet, Saez & Zucman) — equal-split adults, year-end, 1976–2024

US wealth shares from RealTime Inequality (Blanchet, Saez & Zucman) — a monthly distributional national accounts series. Shown here as the December value of each year, 1976–2024, for equal-split adults.

This is an independent estimate of the same wealth concentration the WID chart shows; the top 1% wealth share here reaches about 37% by 2019. Use the two side by side as a robustness check.

Source provenance: built from a user-supplied Full Database.xlsx whose structure (monthly DINA, equal-split adults, fine top groups, 1976→) is the RealTime Inequality database. Annual = December value; capped at 2024 to exclude the nowcast tail.
