Are manufacturers' input costs and capex plans moving together?

Average z-scores across the five regional Fed manufacturing surveys — New York, Philadelphia, Richmond, Dallas, Kansas City — monthly, 2005-01 to 2026-06

What's plotted: two composite series built from the five regional Federal Reserve manufacturing surveys. Input costs = each survey's current prices-paid indicator; Capex plans = each survey's future (~6-months-ahead) capital-expenditures indicator. Each region's series is z-scored over its own full published history, then the z-scores are averaged across the regions reporting that month.

Reading it: 0 = a region-average month at its historical norm; +1 = one standard deviation hotter than normal. Click the dotted legend entries to overlay any single region's z-score.

Scope: the five surveys ask similar but not identical questions. Four price series are seasonally-adjusted diffusion indexes; Richmond's prices-paid series is an annualized percent change (not seasonally adjusted) — z-scoring is what puts them on a comparable scale. Survey histories differ (Philadelphia from 1968, Richmond 1997, NY & Kansas City 2001, Dallas 2004); each z-score uses its own series' full history. The plot starts 2005-01, the first January with all five surveys running.

Solid lines = 5-region averages. Dotted entries (click to show) = individual region z-scores. Drag the slider or use the 1Y/5Y/10Y buttons to zoom. Data through June 2026.

Sources — one line per survey (all values recomputed from the banks' own published historical files)

  • New York Fed Empire State Manufacturing Survey, seasonally-adjusted all-series CSV, columns PPCDISA (prices paid, current, diffusion index SA) and CEFDISA (future capital expenditures, diffusion index SA). Survey page: overview & historical data.
  • Philadelphia Fed Manufacturing Business Outlook Survey, SA diffusion-index history CSV, columns PPC (prices paid, current) and CEF (future capital expenditures). Survey page: MBOS.
  • Richmond Fed Fifth District Survey of Manufacturing Activity, historical data XLSX, columns nsa_mfg_pct_chg_prcs_pd_c (prices paid, annualized % change, NSA) and sa_mfg_capital_expnd_e (expected capital expenditures, diffusion index SA). Survey page: manufacturing survey.
  • Dallas Fed Texas Manufacturing Outlook Survey, all-data SA workbook, columns prm (prices paid for raw materials, SA) and fcexp (future capital expenditures, SA). Survey page: TMOS data.
  • Kansas City Fed Tenth District Survey of Manufacturers, historical monthly data XLSX (Table 2), rows “Prices paid for raw materials” under Versus a Month Ago (seasonally adjusted) and “Capital expenditures” under Expected in Six Months (seasonally adjusted). Survey page: manufacturing survey.
How this was produced — no pixel-tracing; every value recomputed from the five banks' published historical data files (retrieved 2026-07-04, data through June 2026).
Transformation steps
  • Downloaded each bank's full-history data file (links above); raw files kept untouched.
  • For each region, took the current prices-paid indicator (“input costs”) and the future capital-expenditures indicator (“capex plans”).
  • Z-scored each region-series over its own full published history: z = (x − mean) / sample std-dev.
  • Averaged the available regional z-scores in each month (equal weights; a region missing a month is simply left out of that month's average).
  • Spot-checked parsed values against the banks' published June 2026 releases (e.g. Empire prices paid 61.0; Dallas raw-materials prices 42.4) and validated the recomputation against the previous vintage of this chart (correlation 1.000).
