Effective Federal Tax Rates by Income Group

Four research teams' estimates compared, 2014 (Piketty & Saez: 2004)

This chart recreates Figure 1 of David Splinter's 2020 Brookings comment on Saez & Zucman's "Progressive Wealth Taxation." It compares effective federal tax rates by income group as estimated by four research teams that use different income definitions, tax measures, and sharing units:

Piketty & Saez (2007) — fiscal income; payroll and corporate taxes (2004 data). CBO — market income plus social insurance benefits. Tax Policy Center (TPC) — expanded cash income. Auten & Splinter (AS) — pre-tax/after-transfer national income; includes state corporate and estate taxes; uses larger low-end bins (P0–50, P50–90).

Income on the x-axis is grouped by percentile of the income distribution (P0–20 = bottom 20%, Top 0.01% = the highest 1-in-10,000). Toggle between excluding and including federal payroll taxes.

Sources — every line on this chart, with exact citation

  • TPC: Tax Policy Center microsimulation model, 2014 estimates. taxpolicycenter.org/model-estimates.

How this chart was produced. The values plotted are transcribed verbatim from the CSV that ships with Splinter's Figure 1 master data file — every bar is a number from that table, not a pixel measurement off the published chart image.
