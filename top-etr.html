IRS SOI — Effective federal income tax rate by AGI percentile

Compiled directly from IRS Statistics of Income tables, 2015 and 2020

This chart shows the IRS's own narrow definition of an effective tax rate: federal individual income tax after credits divided by Adjusted Gross Income, for cumulative percentile bins of AGI (Top 0.001%, Top 0.01%, ..., Top 50%, All). Data is direct from the IRS SOI Individual Statistical Tables by Tax Rate and Income Percentile, compiled into the source PDF here.

Two years are plotted on the percentile basis (2015 and 2020). The source PDF also contains older years (1945, 1955, 1965, 1975, 1985, 1995) but in a different income-bin format (by AGI dollar size, not by percentile) — those are not directly comparable to the percentile series and are omitted here.

Scope note. This is the narrowest ETR measure on the dashboard — federal individual income tax only, as % of AGI. It excludes payroll, corporate, state & local. AGI is also narrower than CBO/PSZ income concepts (excludes employer-paid FICA, some retirement contributions, transfers). For that reason, IRS SOI rates here may look higher than CBO's federal rates and much higher than PSZ's all-government rates for the same group — different numerator, different denominator. Compare like-for-like.

X-axis is cumulative ("Top 1%" = the highest-AGI 1%, "Top 50%" = the highest-AGI 50%). Hover for exact rate.

Sources — every point on this chart, with exact citation

  • IRS SOI: Internal Revenue Service Statistics of Income, "Individual Statistical Tables by Tax Rate and Income Percentile." Specifically: "Number of Returns, Shares of AGI and Total Income Tax, AGI Floor on Percentiles in Current and Constant Dollars, and Average Tax Rates. Classified by: Selected Descending Cumulative Percentiles of Returns Based on Income Size Using the Definition of AGI for Each Year." irs.gov/statistics.
  • 2020 data: Page 1 of top-etr_3.pdf in this repo. Dated "8-May-23" in the PDF header — IRS publication date for tax year 2020 data.
  • 2015 data: Pages 2–3 of top-etr_3.pdf. Dated "28-Jul-XX" in the PDF header.
  • Source PDF: The aggregator PDF (filename top-etr_3.pdf) compiles IRS SOI individual-statistics tables across multiple years. The cited IRS table is the canonical source.
How this chart was produced. PDF text extracted with pdfplumber, then parsed row-by-row with a regex on the percentile labels. No tracing.
Show transformation steps
  • Parsed pages of .raw/top-etr/top-etr_3.pdf for year header (20YY Effective Tax Rate) and per-row rate values.
  • Kept only the percentile-based years (2015, 2020) for cross-year comparability.
  • Older years (1945–1995) use an AGI-dollar-bin format and require a separate transform; out of scope for this chart.
  • Output: data/top-etr/etr-by-agi-percentile.csv + metadata.json.
