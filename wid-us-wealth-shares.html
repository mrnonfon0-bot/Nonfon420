US net personal wealth shares: top 10%, 1%, bottom 50%

World Inequality Database — net personal wealth, equal-split adults, 1979–2024

Share of total net personal wealth (assets minus debts) held by the top 10%, top 1%, and bottom 50% of US adults, from the World Inequality Database (equal-split adults).

The top 10% hold about 71% of all wealth and the top 1% about a third. The bottom half of the country holds almost none — its share even turned negative from 2007–2017, meaning the bottom 50% collectively owed more than they owned.

WID/DINA net personal wealth, equal-split adults. Levels differ across wealth sources (Federal Reserve DFA, Saez–Zucman, RealTime Inequality) by method; compare the RealTime Inequality wealth chart in this gallery for a second source.
